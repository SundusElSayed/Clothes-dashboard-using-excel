#  Clothes Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Clothes Store Sales Analysis Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, customer demographics, product categories, order trends, and sales distribution across different channels and states.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful insights, helping stakeholders understand customer behavior, identify top-performing product categories, and monitor business performance.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

![Clothes Store Analysis Dashboard](https://github.com/SundusElSayed/Clothes-dashboard-using-excel/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-10-19%20210037.png?raw=true)

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and order volume.
- Analyze customer demographics by gender and age group.
- Identify the best-performing clothing categories.
- Track monthly order trends and seasonal patterns.
- Compare product distribution across sales channels.
- Analyze geographical sales performance across Indian states.
- Explore order statuses, including delivered, cancelled, refunded, and returned orders.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Customer Demographics
- Orders distribution by gender.
- Sales distribution across age groups.
- Customer segmentation into Teenagers, Adults, and Seniors.

### 2. Product Category Analysis
- Sales comparison across clothing categories.
- Number of orders by category.
- Identification of top-performing categories based on sales and order volume.

### 3. Monthly Order Trends
- Monthly order volume throughout the year.
- Identification of peak and low-performing months.
- Analysis of changes in order activity over time.

### 4. Sales Channel Analysis
- Comparison of items sold across different sales channels.
- Performance analysis for Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Nalli, Meesho, and other channels.
- Identification of channels contributing the highest item volume.

### 5. Geographical Analysis
- Visualization of order distribution across Indian states.
- Identification of geographical areas with higher order volumes.

### 6. Order Status Analysis
- Interactive filtering by order status.
- Analysis of delivered, cancelled, refunded, and returned orders.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- **Customer demographics:** Women account for 69% of orders, while men account for 31%.
- **Age segmentation:** Adults represent 50% of sales, followed by Teenagers at 30% and Seniors at 20%.
- **Top sales channel:** Amazon leads with 11,075 items sold, followed by Myntra with 7,298 and Flipkart with 6,750.
- **Monthly trends:** March records the highest monthly order volume at 2,819 orders.
- **Product performance:** The Set category leads in sales and order volume among the displayed categories.
- **Overall performance:** The store recorded 31,047 orders and total sales of 21,176,377.

*Note: These insights reflect the figures displayed in the dashboard and are subject to the underlying dataset and its definitions.*
